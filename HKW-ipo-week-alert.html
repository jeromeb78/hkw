One of the most anticipated IPOs of our time is approaching, and we are studying it together as a live lesson. This is not a recommendation to buy. It is a chance to practice how a wise steward evaluates any opportunity before acting.

Five things to watch
I The final price. Watch whether it prices within, above, or below its range.
II The first-day opening. Shares often open far from the IPO price. A limit order sets the most you are willing to pay.
III Revenue versus losses. Read how fast the company is growing and how much it is spending to grow.
IV The lock-up date. When insiders can sell, often about 180 days later, prices can shift.
V Your own plan. Emergency fund first, and no single stock should dominate your portfolio.
