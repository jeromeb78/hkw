# Partner Links: How They Pay You and Where to Apply

*Prepared November 4, 2026. The live, editable version is at https://claude.ai/artifact/8jpPFSqCdj2gJ9qGVvzpti*

Clicking a link in the app does not pay you on its own. You earn only after you are approved for each partner's affiliate program and Jerome places your personal tracking link behind that partner's button.

## How partner links pay you

1. **Open a publisher account** with each network the partner uses (Impact, CJ, FlexOffers, and others below).
2. **Apply to each partner** inside that network. Financial brands review your site and content before approving you; approval can take days or weeks, and some decline new partners.
3. **Receive your tracking links.**
4. **Give the links to Jerome,** who places each one behind that partner's button.
5. **Get paid by the network,** usually monthly once you pass its minimum payout, after you submit a W-9 and payment details.

## Rules to follow

- **Disclose on every paid card,** right beside the link.
- **Broker pay stays flat:** a set fee per account opened or funded, never tied to trades or account size. Confirm as the attorney.
- **Follow each partner's terms:** disclosure wording, pre-approval of rate content, no paid ads on their brand names.
- **Never promise results.**
- **Keep records** of approvals, agreements, and payouts.
